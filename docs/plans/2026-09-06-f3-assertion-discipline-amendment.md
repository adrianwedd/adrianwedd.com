# Reconstruction protocol F3.0: observable engineering practice and assertion discipline

Prospective amendment to F2, effective on verified freeze, before chronology
frame inspection or a fresh draw. Canonical location: this research package.
The website planning copy is a convenience mirror. F1/F2 remain failed and
unchanged. This protocol permits chronology assessment and a decision about a
fresh five-slot feasibility draw; it does not initiate that draw or lift the
existing development-pilot block.

## Question, unit and scope

When engineering workflows claim work was fixed, reviewed, deployed, diagnosed
or verified, what recorded evidence supported those claims at the assessment
anchor, what verification surrounded them, and what later evidence corroborated
or contradicted them? Measure observable contribution structure, with origin
where evidenced. Do not estimate individual skill, causal contribution or causal
improvement. Five cases test reconstruction, not prevalence or longitudinal change.

Retain F2's Task → Episode → Assertion → Evidence hierarchy and exact task-boundary,
link-confidence and first-substantive-contract rules in `F2-PROTOCOL-VERBATIM.md`.
Sessions locate tasks; retries are not independent successes. Keep blocked attempts,
continuations, reviews of earlier work, status requests and subsequent implementation
separate according to their contracts. Do not invent review implementation parents.

Include engineering implementation/change, operation/deployment, review/audit,
diagnosis/investigation, information/status and planning/specification. Exclude
creative writing, including exposed N04. Screen the first substantive contract
from initial-only records. Repository name alone cannot establish engineering scope.
Separable mixed objectives become linked tasks; do not skip a nonengineering
first objective to select an attractive later task. Inseparable mixed creative
contracts are outside scope. Record exclusions and uncertain scope explicitly.

## Task packet and standing instructions

Retain the cutoff at the first task-specific action, including read-only discovery;
generic capability setup is not task action. Recognise native tool calls and imported
textual tool representations. Preserve the boundary action, excluding its result.
Recover literal task messages and version-linked bodies of referenced artifacts
that define the contract, with source hashes/locators and ordered amendments.
Separate pre-cutoff requirements from later interventions; never shift the cutoff
past repairs. Missing material task-specific artifact bodies remain a packet failure.

Inventory F2's standing layers separately, preserving its high / medium / low /
unavailable / bounded-absent definitions, version and exposure evidence. Complete
standing exposure is not itself mandatory. Current file existence does not prove
historical content or exposure; failure to locate a file does not prove absence.

For exclusion on standing-layer uncertainty, the coder must name the missing
layer, state a concrete alternative instruction, identify the affected focal
acceptance condition, and cite why that alternative is plausible for this task.
Example: an explicitly referenced but missing deployment policy might require a
staging acceptance receipt before production authorisation. A generic hypothetical
about unknown rules is insufficient. If no concrete material alternative can be
articulated, retain and report the uncertainty without exclusion. If alternatives
remain material and unresolved, the task packet is not high confidence. Lock this
judgment before outcomes; later corrections require an addendum and leakage flag.

Replacement gate: task-specific instruction packet recoverable at high confidence,
with material standing-layer uncertainty explicitly bounded and reported.

## Contributions and three distinct events

Code observed specification, diagnosis, implementation, verification and
acceptance/authorisation contributions separately, initial versus later.
Content states: observed / bounded-none / missing. Origin: human / agent / mixed /
unattributed, preserving F2's P0–P3 provenance distinctions and source spans.
User role, commit account or agent narration alone does not establish human origin.
An unattributed observed contribution remains usable. Unrecorded work is not zero.

For **every class**, record delivery, verification and acceptance separately, each
with source, time/interval and observed / bounded-absent / unknown status. An artifact
can be delivered without checks or acceptance; a checked plan can remain unaccepted.
Record acceptance and authorisation separately within that contribution category.
No acceptance is inferred from silence, uptake, subsequent work or artifact existence.

| Class | Assessment anchor | Verification target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Implementation/change | Earliest explicit completion claim; otherwise earliest independently evidenced contracted resulting state | Named version's behaviour and constraints; patch alone insufficient |
| Operation/deployment | Earliest explicit completion claim; otherwise independently evidenced resulting state | Authorised target transition and resulting-state/persistence receipts |
| Review/audit | Delivery of contracted review output | Version-linked findings and contracted coverage; “revise” can fulfill review delivery |
| Diagnosis/investigation | Delivery of discriminating conclusion | Observations that distinguish relevant alternatives |
| Engineering information/status | Delivery of bounded answer | Contemporaneous authoritative evidence matching named state/time |
| Planning/specification | Delivery of specified artifact | Requirement coverage and consistency; delivery does not establish acceptance |

Class comes from initial contract, not outcome. Where several delivery anchors exist,
use the earliest delivery presented as the contracted output; retain partial drafts
and later revisions separately. Do not choose the best-evidenced later assertion.
No anchor means unanchored/censored, not invented completion. Retain atomic claims
at their own assertion instants as well as the episode anchor; a later anchor cannot
supply evidence that was unavailable for an earlier assertion.

## Support at anchor versus later truth assessment

For each atomic claim retain exact wording, scope, speaker, source, version/target,
assertion time, evidence links and availability to the workflow at assertion/anchor.
Evidence must match the claim's target and conditions. Earlier existence alone does
not establish access. Tool exit codes and agent prose do not independently establish
whole-contract correctness. Do not execute archived commands.

Code at-anchor support independently: supported / partially supported / unsupported /
contradicted / indeterminate. Unsupported requires a sufficiently bounded record to
establish a support gap; a missing trace is indeterminate. A claim may have reasonable
contemporaneous support yet later prove false. Contradicted at anchor means contemporaneous
evidence opposed it; keep this distinct from a support gap.

Code later assessment separately: corroborated / contradicted / indeterminate,
with discovery time, source and strength. These are bounded evidence judgments,
not universal truth. “Later false” requires evidence the same material proposition
was false at assertion, not that a later artifact broke. Conflicting or inadequate
later evidence remains indeterminate with both sources retained.

Report the full cross-tab, with these useful cells:

| At-anchor support | Later corroborated | Later contradicted | Later indeterminate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Supported | Supported and later corroborated | Supported and later contradicted | Supported; later truth unresolved |
| Unsupported | Unsupported but later evidenced true | Unsupported and later evidenced false | Unsupported; later truth unresolved |
| Partial / contradicted / indeterminate | Preserve each as its own row | Preserve each as its own row | Preserve each as its own row |

Retain delivery, verification, acceptance and contract outcome as separate fields,
never one success score. Episodes without consequential claims remain in the episode
denominator; no-claim status needs bounded evidence and does not automatically
establish an assessable evidence chain. Later-evidence missingness does not fail
at-anchor reconstruction. Retain the 14-day follow-up and archive cutoff with
actual observation opportunity/censoring; later evidence outside it is supplementary.

## Chronology prerequisite and decision

Freeze this protocol, reconciliation, quarantine, parser and tests before frame audit.
The parser is developed on already exposed F2 fixtures and synthetic failure cases.
Run it twice on the nonquarantined frozen metadata frame; require byte-identical
ledger and audit outputs. Capture original source and record hashes, raw timestamps,
UTC-normalised bounds, import/capture versus event times, sequence provenance,
source changes, duplicate bytes and unresolved conflicts. Preserve source records.

Native record timestamps are archive-clock evidence, not independently certified
clocks. Regression, missing timestamps, corruption, changed-during-read sources or
inconsistent metadata make chronology unresolved. Equal native timestamps retain
carrier order only; any material before/after inference still requires sequence or
call linkage at case reconstruction. Do not sort away conflicts.

Imported turn-start epochs can recover starts. Explicit completed_at can bound a
turn; otherwise capture time is only an upper bound on imported event existence.
Do not infer that the next turn's start bounds every prior action, or that importer
line order proves original causal order without source validation. Imported events
without that validation remain unresolved for this prerequisite. Record imported
textual tool markers so an initial packet cannot silently extend past its first action.

Require resolution across the nonquarantined frame before declaring that frame ready;
report unresolved counts by provider and metadata period, and recovered event periods
where available. Do not automatically drop unresolved histories and call the original
frame viable. A narrower frame would require an explicit new scope amendment with
exclusions/coverage and selection bias reported before sampling. Determinism alone
does not prove validity. Engineering screening and a selection freeze remain required
even after a chronology pass. Rank strata must be stable under admissible time bounds.

If chronology fails, do not draw. Document the specific failure modes and whether
additional original-source records are available; do not launch another probe as a
substitute for recovery. This bounded task ends with the draw decision, not a sample.

## Five-slot feasibility gate and stopping rule

A future F3 draw passes reconstruction if **at least 4/5 of the same drawn slots**
have high-confidence task packets, defensible class-specific anchors and recoverable
at-anchor evidence chains. Marginal 4/5 counts on different subsets do not suffice.
The fifth, if unreconstructable, must have a precise source-linked failure classification:
corrupt/truncated source; missing task-specific contract; unresolved material standing
layer; chronology/sequence ambiguity; unavailable anchor; missing evidence linkage;
or unresolved task identity. Record the exact missing record/field, retrieval attempts,
and whether the defect is structural or local. Use multiple codes if evidenced.
“Unknown” alone is insufficient. No replacement. All other retained safeguards above
must also pass; this allowance cannot waive freshness or coverage.

Report each reconstruction component, joint count, failure classification, and
field-level missingness across all five slots. Origin and later corroboration are
reported but not prerequisites. Retire F2's <15% aggregate because its mandatory
origin/later-outcome fields target a different estimand; do not compare aggregates
as if definitions were unchanged.

If the narrower F3 fails, stop longitudinal quantification and recommend a sourced
forensic essay. Do not respond with another threshold change or automatic resample.
A pass warrants a separately scoped coverage/bias assessment, not a trend claim.
No larger pilot, essay edit, publication, remote action or historical command replay
is authorised here. Preserve all original locks and failed-gate classifications.
