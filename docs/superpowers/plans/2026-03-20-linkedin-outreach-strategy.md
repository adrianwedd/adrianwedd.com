# LinkedIn Outreach Strategy — Adrian Wedd

> **For execution:** This is a strategy and content plan, not a code implementation plan. Execute in a dedicated session with Sales Navigator active. The 30-day trial clock matters — start when ready to execute, not before.

**Goal:** Convert the newly character-aligned LinkedIn profile into a lead generation channel for consulting work, using the 30-day Sales Navigator Core free trial (50 InMail credits).

**Brand position:** "I build things that work for people who need them — and break things on purpose to make sure they do."

**Voice rule:** Every piece of outreach must pass the STYLE.md calibration test. No achievement listing, no third-person framing, no buzzwords, no hedging without epistemic purpose. First-person immediate. Direct.

## Phase 2: Content Strategy (Weeks 1-4, ongoing)

### Purpose

LinkedIn content serves three functions:
1. **Warm the audience** before InMail outreach (they've seen your thinking)
2. **Demonstrate expertise** without pitching (let the work speak)
3. **Give prospects a reason to click through** to adrianwedd.com/services

### Content Calendar — First 4 Weeks

Post 2-3 times per week. Each post should be one idea, 150-300 words, in Adrian's voice. No hashtag spam. No "I'm delighted to announce." No engagement bait.

**Week 1: Establish the character**

- [ ] **Post 1 — Introduction / repositioning.** "I just rewrote my entire LinkedIn profile. The old one was a keyword-stuffed pipe-separated tag list. The new one starts with: 'I build things that work for people who need them.' Here's why I changed it." Brief story of the brand exercise. Link to adrianwedd.com/services.
- [ ] **Post 2 — The Greenpeace through-line.** "The most useful thing I learned wasn't a technology. It was planning operations against well-resourced opponents at Greenpeace. The optimistic plan is the dangerous plan. That thinking followed me into cybersecurity, then AI safety. It hasn't changed." No link needed — character-building.
- [ ] **Post 3 — Repurpose "Why Demonstrated Risk Is Ignored."** Extract the core thesis in 200 words. Link to the full blog post.

**Week 2: Demonstrate expertise**

- [ ] **Post 4 — Failure First teaser.** "I tested 120 AI models with 18,000 adversarial prompts. The models that performed best in benchmarks were often the worst at handling real-world failure modes. Here's what I mean." 2-3 specific findings. Link to failurefirst.org.
- [ ] **Post 5 — The AuDHD advantage.** "I'm Autistic and ADHD. I name this because it directly explains why I'm good at what I do." Brief version of the about page positioning. This post exists because it's true, not for engagement — but authentic neurodivergent representation in tech is rare, and it will resonate.
- [ ] **Post 6 — Repurpose "When AI Systems Talk to Each Other, Safety Breaks Down."** Core finding in 200 words. Link to blog post.

**Week 3: Show the work**

- [ ] **Post 7 — Wolf Clan case study.** "I built a full operations platform for a local martial arts club. 79 lesson plans, JWT member portal, live kanban, zero build tools. Runs on Cloudflare edge. Here's what I learned about building for community organisations." Link to project page.
- [ ] **Post 8 — Repurpose "The AI Productivity J-Curve."** Enterprise AI failure patterns. Relevant to ICP 2.
- [ ] **Post 9 — SPARK story.** "I'm building a robot for my neurodivergent children. Not a toy — a non-coercive AI companion that uses declarative presence instead of commands. Here's why." Link to spark.wedd.au.

**Week 4: Convert**

- [ ] **Post 10 — The sovereign AI angle.** "If your organisation can't send data to US clouds, you still need AI capabilities. I build data-sovereign RAG, self-hosted inference, and local-first agentic systems. Here's what that looks like in practice." Targets ICP 2 (government, healthcare, legal).
- [ ] **Post 11 — Repurpose "Safety-First Therapeutic AI."** Relevant to healthcare ICP 1.
- [ ] **Post 12 — Direct CTA.** "I'm taking on new consulting work. AI safety evaluation, systems architecture, web development for regulated industries. Based in Tasmania, working remotely. If you have a problem worth solving, tell me what it is. adrianwedd.com/services"

### Content Voice Rules

Every post must pass these checks:
- **First-person immediate?** "I built" not "We delivered"
- **Specific?** Name the project, the number, the finding — not generic claims
- **No achievement listing?** Show the thinking, not the credential
- **Would Adrian actually say this?** If it sounds like a LinkedIn influencer, delete it
- **One idea per post?** Not a thread of 12 listicle items
- **Link in first comment, not post body.** LinkedIn suppresses posts with external links in the body. Write the full insight in the post, then add "Full post: [link]" as the first comment.

### InMail Rules

1. **Always personalise the first line.** Reference something specific about their profile, company, or a post they wrote. Never send a template cold.
2. **One InMail per prospect.** No follow-up InMails. If they don't respond, they're not interested. Move on. (Note: InMail credits are returned if the recipient responds within 90 days — even negatively. Effective InMails are essentially free.)
3. **No attachments, no pitch decks.** Link to adrianwedd.com/services or a specific blog post.
4. **Send Tuesday-Thursday, 9am-11am AEST.** Highest open rates.
5. **Track in a simple spreadsheet.** Name, company, ICP tier, date sent, open rate, response (Y/N), outcome.
6. **Profile browsing as outreach.** When you view profiles via Sales Navigator, prospects see "Adrian Wedd viewed your profile." This is free visibility. Browse deliberately — 10-15 ICP profiles per day.

## Phase 4: Connection Request Strategy

Separate from InMail — connection requests are free and unlimited (with daily caps).

### Who to connect with

- **Prospects before InMailing them.** A connection request with a note is warmer than a cold InMail. Send the connection request first; if accepted, follow up with a message (not InMail — saves credits).
- **People who engage with your posts.** Anyone who likes, comments, or shares — send a connection request.
- **People in your ICP industries.** Build the network before you need it.
- **Peers in AI safety / adversarial evaluation.** Not prospects — but visibility in the field.

### Connection Request Note (300 char max)

```
Hi [name] — I work on AI safety evaluation and systems
architecture for regulated industries. Saw [specific thing].
Would value the connection. Adrian
```

Keep it under 300 chars. No pitch. No "I'd love to pick your brain." Just a direct, honest reason to connect.

## Phase 5: Engagement Strategy (Ongoing)

### Daily habit (15 minutes)

- [ ] **Comment on 3-5 posts** from people in your ICPs or AI safety peers. Not "Great post!" — add a specific thought, observation, or question. In your voice.
- [ ] **Respond to all comments on your posts** within 24 hours. Conversations in comments have higher visibility than the post itself.
- [ ] **Check "Who Viewed Your Profile"** (Sales Navigator gives full list). If someone from an ICP company viewed you, send a connection request.

### What to comment on

- AI governance announcements from government bodies
- Healthcare digital transformation posts
- AI safety discussions (you have genuine expertise here — use it)
- Posts from people who are clearly struggling with a problem you solve

### What NOT to do

- Don't comment on every post from the same person (looks like stalking)
- Don't pitch in comments (ever)
- Don't use LinkedIn engagement pods or automation tools
- Don't post more than once per day (dilutes signal)
- Don't use hashtags like a 2019 LinkedIn guru (#AI #Innovation #Disruption)

## Key Reminders

1. **The profile is the landing page.** Every InMail, connection request, and comment drives someone back to the profile. The profile now has character — let it do the work.
2. **Lead with value, not with pitch.** Share thinking, not services. The services page exists for when they're ready.
3. **50 InMails in 30 days is plenty.** Quality over quantity. One well-researched InMail to the right person beats 10 spray-and-pray messages.
4. **Your blog is your content library.** You have 23 published posts. That's 4+ months of LinkedIn content without writing anything new.
5. **Do it properly or don't bother.** If you're going to do LinkedIn outreach, do it in your voice. The moment it sounds like everyone else's outreach, it stops working.
