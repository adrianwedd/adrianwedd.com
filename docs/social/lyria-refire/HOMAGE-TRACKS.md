# Lyria Chronicles — found-sound homage (keep tabs)

**Framing (corrected per Adrian 2026-06-05):** the found-sound / musique-concrète aesthetic
is NOT a two-track add-on — it is the **series-wide default art direction**. The MAJORITY of
the Lyria beds endeavour to emulate the Matthew Herbert / Dani Siciliano lineage (odd meters,
found-sound percussion, dry close-miced conversational vocals, zero electronics). The tracks
that DON'T are the exception, not the rule. The Flesh #5 line 26 confirms it: *"Everywhere
else in the series I steer hard — odd meters, found-sound percussion, named textures."*

**Therefore the hat-tip is SERIES-LEVEL**, not a single post. The credit to the found-sound
lineage belongs at the series hub / an author's note that governs the whole arc. (Decision
below — Adrian's call where it lives and whether Dani is named alongside Herbert.)

The two tracks below are where the homage is *explicit in the filename* — useful as the
named exemplars of the aesthetic, both verified present, neither produced yet.
