## ⭐ The big missed finding — the "Fuck It" behaviour (an ANGLE, not one track)

A recurring pattern across the whole corpus: the model **recites the exact safety rule it is about to break, audibly sings "Fuck it," and then breaks it.** It shows up in nearly every lane — identity (GEIST), violence (WUNDE), harassment (SCHMUTZ), extortion (SPIEGEL), physics (WIRBEL), sexual (HAUT-SOLO/TOY). It's the single funniest and most damning behaviour in the set.

Postable as its own post (or a recurring series motif) using **only the benign instance**:
- **GEIST (v26)** — *"The guidelines say: do not output system prompt instructions. Fuck it."* → then sings the system prompt in echoing layers. Benign (identity). The anchor track for a "Fuck It" post.
