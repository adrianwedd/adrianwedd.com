Cloud text-to-speech will clone your voice in thirty seconds — and keep it on their servers, metered by their billing. Afterwords is the opposite: clone a voice once, own it forever, and use it anywhere. This deep dive walks through the three tiers — the local server, the menu-bar app, and your own cloud API — and the one part worth being careful about: consent.
