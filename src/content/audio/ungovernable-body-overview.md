_The Ungovernable Body_ is a near-future feminist techno-thriller about biological governance and the right to finitude — and an experiment in whether a serious dramatic film can be produced by a team of AI agents operating a git repository under strict human-authored constraints.

This deep dive covers the repo-as-studio architecture: story facts as structured YAML with a claims discipline that forbids presenting speculation as fact, twenty-three architecture decision records governing material choices, dialogue machine-validated against the screenplay token by token, character consistency treated as a locking problem, and swarm QA rounds auditing over a thousand generated clips for identity drift and hallucinated speech.
