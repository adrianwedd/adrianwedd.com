_The Right to Rot_ is the proof-of-concept short at the centre of _The Ungovernable Body_ — a near-future feminist techno-thriller about biological governance and the right to finitude. This deep dive steps inside its world rather than its pipeline.

It covers the machinery of Continuity — the system that keeps a citizen's functions running through "biological uncertainty" by maintaining an authorized digital proxy, the Twin, that outranks the volatile living Principal. It walks the Halo, the wrist-worn band that prices future risk onto the present and flips green to red when the actuarial math turns against a body; the way refusal is absorbed as evidence of impairment; and the copper-room resistance built plate by plate beneath a city that never bothered to look down. Underneath all of it, the title's claim: the right to remain uncounted, to age without intervention, to permit your own deletion.
